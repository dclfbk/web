Digital Commons Lab is a research unit of FBK's Digital Society Center. Our work moves between policy, communities and technology: studying how digital systems affect society, then building practical ways to govern them better.

### Govern responsibly
Frameworks, evidence and practical tools for transparent, fair and accountable AI and data use.

### Open knowledge
Open data, maps and civic technologies that people and institutions can inspect, reuse and improve.

### Work with communities
Participatory research that combines local knowledge, public institutions and technical expertise.

### Make complexity visible
Data visualisation and digital twins that turn evidence into a shared basis for decisions.
