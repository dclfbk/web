We welcome students who are eager to get involved and build something that interests both them and us. An internship can begin with one of our proposals or with your own idea and develop into a thesis, a scientific publication, a project or a new digital commons.

If this way of working appeals to you, [contact us](#contact) and tell us what you would like to do.
